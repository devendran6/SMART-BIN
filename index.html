Physical Hardware & IoT Integration

How to connect physical HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors and servo lids to this live dashboard

Physical Dustbin Hardware Architecture

1. Ultrasonic Sensors (HC-SR04) are installed under the motorized lid of each dustbin pointing downward.
2. The sensor emits sound pulses at 40 kHz, reflects off the waste top layer, and calculates distance.
3. Touchless Opening: An infrared sensor (TCRT5000 or PIR) triggers an SG90 / MG996R servo motor to open the lid touchlessly.
4. Fill % Formula: Fill% = ((TotalBinHeight - Distance) / TotalBinHeight) * 100

Compostable Dustbin Pinout
  • Ultrasonic TRIG → GPIO 5
  • Ultrasonic ECHO → GPIO 18
  • Lid Servo Motor → GPIO 13 (PWM)
  • IR Proximity Sensor → GPIO 14
Decomposable Dustbin Pinout
  • Ultrasonic TRIG → GPIO 19
  • Ultrasonic ECHO → GPIO 21
  • Lid Servo Motor → GPIO 22 (PWM)
  • IR Proximity Sensor → GPIO 23
